Your Questions, Answered

What does "human-grade" actually mean?

It means every ingredient in our recipes is sourced, handled, and processed to the same safety standards as food made for people. Our kitchen is USDA-certified for human food production - the same facility could legally produce meals for a restaurant.

Can I customize recipes for my cat's allergies or health conditions?

Yes. During the quiz, you can flag specific proteins, grains, or ingredients to avoid. Our nutritionists build your plan around those restrictions. If your cat has a diagnosed condition (kidney disease, diabetes, IBD), we recommend sharing the plan with your vet.

How does shipping work?

We ship in insulated, recyclable boxes with dry ice. Meals arrive fresh, not frozen. Delivery is free on all orders and subscriptions. Most orders arrive within 3-5 business days.

What if my cat doesn't like it?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on your first order. If your cat turns their nose up (it happens), contact us and we will refund you in full. No returns needed - donate the remaining food to a local shelter.

Is this food AAFCO-compliant?

Every PurrFresh recipe meets AAFCO nutrient profiles for adult cats and kittens. We publish full nutritional analysis on each recipe page, including guaranteed analysis, caloric content, and ingredient sourcing.

Can I pause or cancel my subscription?

Anytime, with one click in your account dashboard. No cancellation fees, no phone calls, no guilt trips. Pause for a month or cancel permanently - your choice.
